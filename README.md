# Awesome-Augmented-Analytics

# Top Augmented Analytics Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on AI-Assisted BI, Natural Language Query, Auto-Insights, Metric Layers & Agentic Analytics*  
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Augmented Analytics**. These systems combine traditional BI with AI—natural language questions, automated insights, anomaly detection, and governed metrics—so more people can get answers without writing SQL.

**Examples** include ThoughtSpot, Sisense Fusion, Qlik AutoML, Power BI Copilot, Tableau Pulse, Tellius, Sisu Data, Oracle Analytics Cloud, SAP Analytics Cloud, and Domo AI (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Classic open BI (**Metabase**, **Apache Superset**, **Lightdash**) is adding AI assistants and agentic workflows. This section lists the strongest open analytics foundations and emerging AI-augmented projects.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[ThoughtSpot](https://www.thoughtspot.com/)**  
  Search- and AI-driven analytics platform—natural language queries, Liveboards, and Spotter-style agents on governed data.

- **[Power BI Copilot, Tableau Pulse, Sisense Fusion, Qlik](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/)**  
  Major BI vendors embedding copilots, automated insights, and autoML into enterprise analytics suites.

- **[Tellius, Sisu Data](https://www.tellius.com/)**  
  Augmented analytics specialists focused on why-change analysis, decision intelligence, and AI-generated insights.

- **[Oracle Analytics Cloud, SAP Analytics Cloud, Domo AI](https://www.oracle.com/analytics/)**  
  Cloud analytics platforms with AI assistants, predictive features, and enterprise semantic layers.

- **[Other commercial augmented analytics platforms](https://www.thoughtspot.com/)**  
  Additional decision-intelligence and conversational BI products.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Metabase](https://github.com/metabase/metabase)**  
  Leading open-source BI—self-service questions, dashboards, and growing AI/assistant capabilities; easy for business users.

- **[Apache Superset](https://github.com/apache/superset)**  
  Open-source modern data exploration and visualization—SQL IDE, dashboards, and extensible semantic layers at scale.

- **[Lightdash](https://github.com/lightdash/lightdash)**  
  Open-source agentic BI on top of dbt—metrics as code, AI agents, Git/CI workflows for governed analytics.

- **[Evidence, Hex-adjacent open notebooks](https://github.com/evidence-dev/evidence)**  
  Open tools for code-first, narrative analytics and data products.

- **[Redash (community)](https://github.com/getredash/redash)**  
  Open SQL-centric querying and dashboarding still used in many data teams.

- **[Helical Insight (open edition)](https://github.com/helicalinsight/helicalinsight)**  
  Open BI platform with reporting, embedding, and AI assistant modules in community builds.

- **[LLM + warehouse text-to-SQL projects](https://github.com/search?q=text-to-SQL+OR+NLQ+analytics+open+source)**  
  Open agents that translate natural language to SQL against documented schemas—building blocks for augmented analytics.

- **[dbt + metric layers (open)](https://github.com/dbt-labs/dbt-core)**  
  Open transformation and metrics foundations that power governed self-service in Lightdash and similar stacks.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Self-service BI**: Metabase for fastest business-user adoption.
- **Scale exploration**: Superset for analysts and large deployments.
- **Metrics + agents**: Lightdash for dbt-native governed AI analytics.
- **Composable stacks**: Warehouse + dbt + Metabase/Superset/Lightdash + optional LLM NLQ layer.
- Commercial augmented analytics still leads in polished copilots and enterprise semantic governance.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  
**Metabase**, **Superset**, and **Lightdash** are the core open analytics platforms; pair with **dbt** and optional text-to-SQL agents for augmentation.  
Commercial products (ThoughtSpot, Power BI Copilot, Tableau Pulse, Sisense, Tellius, etc.) deliver tighter AI UX and vendor support.  
Many teams self-host open BI and add LLM assistants; enterprises often standardize on commercial augmented analytics. Fully open stacks are production-ready for most mid-market analytics needs.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- AI-generated insights can be wrong or biased. Require human review for material decisions, monitor metric definitions, and control data access. Natural language query does not remove the need for data literacy and governance.
- Open-source tools offer control and cost efficiency but require operations and model/LLM oversight. Commercial platforms shift product and support burden to the vendor. Align analytics AI with your data privacy and quality standards.
